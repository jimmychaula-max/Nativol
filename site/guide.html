CompatibilityTest guide
0.5.0-beta.1 · Intel testing beta

A good first test.

Set up once, then try a small transfer on a backed-up spare NTFS drive.

This is a testing release, not a production-certified driver. Keep a separate verified backup. Apple Silicon work is paused.

Check your setup

  • A native Intel Mac on macOS 15.7.9.
  • Official macFUSE 5.4.0, installed separately with the necessary system approvals.
  • External physical NTFS over USB or Thunderbolt, with a verified single-partition layout and supported 512/4096-byte geometry.
  • A clean volume that passes Nativol's read-only preflight.

Writing is refused outside this scope or when identity, topology, health, or ownership cannot be established. Other filesystems remain managed by macOS. Up to eight Nativol-managed volumes are supported by the beta policy.

Install and approve background access

  1. Obtain the DMG, its SHA-256 checksum, and matching source from the maintainer. Compare the checksum before opening it. Public download links have not been published.
  2. Install official macFUSE 5.4.0 from macFUSE's website and follow its instructions. Nativol does not bundle or install it.
  3. Copy Nativol to Applications and launch it. The beta is ad-hoc signed, not Developer ID signed or notarized. Use only macOS's normal approval flow for a build whose source and checksum you trust; don't disable Gatekeeper.
  4. In Setup, install background access and approve the administrator prompt. If asked, enable the displayed NativolService in Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access.
  5. Enable automatic writing after reviewing the scope. It applies globally to eligible attachments without per-drive enrollment. Enable launch at login if desired.

Administrator approval is for protected setup and updates. Ordinary operations use the service without saving your password. Launch at login runs after you sign in.

Make the first transfer

  1. Connect a backed-up spare NTFS drive. Confirm Nativol reports writable access.
  2. Copy a small folder of synthetic files. Open them, copy them back, and compare checksums where possible.
  3. Eject using Finder. Reconnect, then repeat with Nativol's safe eject action.
  4. Restart, sign in, and check that the menu-bar app returns. Reconnect and confirm writing is enabled without a password.
  5. If Windows is available, read back the files there after a clean eject. File-content checks do not replace a filesystem integrity check.

Stop if copying, ejection, or identity checks fail. Leave the drive connected while safe eject is pending. Nativol does not format or repair a volume, force a busy unmount, or override a dirty or hibernated state.

Share useful results

Tell the person who shared the beta your app version, Intel Mac model family, macOS and macFUSE versions, connection type, approximate drive capacity, and which test steps passed. Include the exact error text with personal details removed.

Do not share full disk images, raw system logs, volume serials, home paths, personal filenames, or credentials. Report suspected privilege or integrity flaws privately; a public vulnerability intake channel has not yet been established.

Source & licenses

Ask the maintainer for the matching 0.5.0-beta.1 source archive with your DMG. The app's original code is MIT licensed. The NTFS engine, health checker and patches retain their applicable GPL licenses; the archive includes their corresponding source. macFUSE is installed separately under its own license. Public repository and release links are being prepared.

What comes next

Independent hardware tests, broader file and lifecycle coverage, a focused privilege-boundary review, and release signing/notarization are needed before a production-readiness claim. Additional macOS versions and Apple Silicon require separate validation.
