BEFORE YOU PLUG IN

The details
worth knowing.

Will it work on my Mac and drive?

This beta enables writing on native Intel macOS 15.7.9 with macFUSE 5.4.0, for eligible external single-partition NTFS drives over USB or Thunderbolt. The helper checks identity, supported geometry and filesystem health. Apple silicon and additional macOS versions are outside this release. See the full requirements.

What happens when I connect a drive?

After background setup, opt in to automatic writing. Nativol checks eligible connected drives and attempts writable mounts. You can keep individual volumes read-only. Unsupported or unhealthy drives are refused; Nativol does not format, repair or force a busy unmount.

Is it really free and open source?

Yes. Nativol's original app code is MIT licensed; the NTFS engine, health checker and patches retain their GPL licenses. macFUSE is installed separately. Donations are optional and do not unlock features. Read the source and licenses on GitHub.

Do my files stay on my Mac?

Yes. Drive operations stay on your Mac. This site has no analytics, external fonts or embedded payment widgets. Donation links and QR codes take you to the relevant payment service; this site does not process payments or receive payment confirmations.
