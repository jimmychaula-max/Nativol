A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you plug in.

Is Nativol free and open source?

Yes, it's free to use. Nativol's original app code is MIT licensed; the NTFS engine, health checker and patches retain their GPL licenses. macFUSE is installed separately. Explore the source on GitHub.

Will it work on every Mac or NTFS drive?

This beta enables writing on native Intel macOS 15.7.9 with macFUSE 5.4.0, for eligible external single-partition NTFS drives over USB or Thunderbolt. The helper checks identity, supported geometry and filesystem health. Apple silicon and additional macOS versions are outside this release.

What does automatic writing mean?

After background setup, enable automatic writing once. Nativol will check eligible connected drives and attempt writable mounts. You can keep individual volumes read-only. Unsupported or unhealthy drives are refused; Nativol does not format, repair or force a busy unmount.

Does Nativol upload my files?

No. Drive operations stay on your Mac. This site has no analytics, external fonts or embedded payment widgets. The Binance Pay QR is an image stored with this site; scanning it opens Binance. Selecting Buy Me a Coffee opens that service's website. Those services handle donations under their own terms; this site does not process payments or receive payment confirmations.

Do I need to donate to use the app?

No. Donations are entirely optional and don't unlock features. Trying the beta on spare media, reporting a useful issue or contributing code are other ways to help.
